# AutoBudgetin v27.5.4 SAFE — Milestone & Completion

Base: v27.5.3 SAFE, which itself keeps the v27.4.0 stable shell/core.

## Credit
- LUNAS state when remaining debt reaches zero.
- New payment button is locked after payoff.
- History can still be viewed, edited, and deleted.
- Editing/deleting a payment can reopen the credit automatically.
- Prevents overpayment beyond the remaining balance.
- One-time congratulations + suggestion to redirect the old monthly installment to savings/emergency fund/next goal.

## Business / Sales
- One-time celebration when a daily sales target is reached.
- One-time celebration when BEP/break-even is reached.
- One-time celebration when the target profit is reached.
- Persistent milestone card gives a practical next-step suggestion.
- Milestones are persisted to avoid repeated popups after reload.

## Stability
- No changes to v24-5-automation.js or v25-features.js.
- Core navigation/data flow remains based on the stable v27.4.0 shell.
